# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Small-business owners, sales and operations leads at SMBs and mid-market teams (fintech, healthcare, logistics, retail, SaaS). They arrive on a laptop during the working day, deciding whether to trust an AI agent with their client follow-ups and CRM. They are busy, sceptical of hype, and need reassurance more than spectacle.

## Product Purpose
Marketing site for Next Higher Solutions (NHS) and its flagship product, NHS Autopilot: an AI agent that schedules and sends reminders and follow-ups, paired with a built-in CRM. The site also sells the team's software, cloud, data/AI and growth services. Success is a visitor booking a call or starting free ("Start free" → /contact).

## Brand Personality
Calm, trustworthy, precise. "It just handles it." Quiet confidence, plenty of air, no urgency tricks. The monospace voice signals engineering rigour; the bright palette signals openness and daylight clarity.

## Anti-references
- Generic SaaS blue-and-purple gradient landing pages.
- Dark "hacker terminal" aesthetics (the previous direction — retired Nov 2026).
- Cream/beige "editorial" backgrounds.
- Hype patterns: countdown timers, fake scarcity, glowing gradient text.

## Design Principles
1. **Reassure, don't dazzle.** Every section should lower the visitor's perceived risk of handing over follow-ups.
2. **Motion explains the flow.** The scroll-driven animation (capture → organize → automate → remind → grow) is the product story; keep it, don't add decoration on top.
3. **One accent, used sparingly.** Colour marks action and state, not decoration.
4. **The logo is the only loud colour.** The site palette stays independent of, and quieter than, the logo.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.2 AA: body text ≥ 4.5:1, large text ≥ 3:1. All animation honours `prefers-reduced-motion` (Lenis disabled, wipes and reveals instant). Visible focus rings everywhere.
